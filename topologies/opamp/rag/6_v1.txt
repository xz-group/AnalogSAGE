The circuit is a three-stage operational amplifier. The first stage consists of a PMOS differential pair with an NMOS folded-cascode active load, the second stage is a non-inverting amplifier comprising a PMOS common-source amplifier driving an NMOS current mirror, and the third stage is an NMOS common-source amplifier with a PMOS current source load, all stabilized by a nested Miller compensation network with a nulling resistor (NMCNR).
