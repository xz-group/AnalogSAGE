The circuit is a three-stage operational amplifier, utilizing an asymmetric NMOS differential pair for the input stage where the inverting branch output is taken from its current-source loaded drain, and the non-inverting branch's drain is tied to the positive supply. This is followed by an intermediate NMOS gain stage that drives a final NMOS common-source output amplifier with a resistive load, with Miller compensation provided by capacitors C0 and C1.
