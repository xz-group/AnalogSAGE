The circuit is a two-stage operational amplifier. The first stage consists of an NMOS differential pair with a cascoded NMOS tail current source, where the `vinp` input's current is folded by a PMOS cascode current mirror, while the `vinn` input directly modulates the global PMOS current mirror reference. The second stage is a common-source NMOS amplifier with a PMOS current source load and Miller compensation, driven by the single-ended output of the first stage.
