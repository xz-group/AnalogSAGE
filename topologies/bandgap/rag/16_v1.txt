The circuit is a voltage-mode bandgap reference, where an opamp forces equal voltages at its inputs, one derived from a unit NPN diode and the other from a series combination of a resistor and an N-times NPN diode. A PMOS current mirror provides operating currents for these core branches and the output, while an additional branch incorporating a third NPN diode and two resistors injects a curvature correction current derived from the input node voltages.
