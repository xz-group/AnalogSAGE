The circuit is a Banba-style current-mode bandgap reference utilizing cascoded PMOS current mirrors, where the generated reference voltage (vref) also serves as the cascode bias. It establishes currents into two branches, one with a unit PNP BJT and composite resistors, and another with an N-times PNP BJT and composite resistors, whose combined operation produces the temperature-compensated vref and a proportional-to-absolute-temperature (PTAT) current (iref).
