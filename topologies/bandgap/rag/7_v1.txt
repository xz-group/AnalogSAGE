The circuit is a PTAT-core bandgap reference where a 5T operational amplifier forces equal voltages at the collectors of a 1x and an 8x PNP transistor, with a resistor in the 8x PNP branch, thereby generating a PTAT current. This PTAT current is mirrored by PMOS transistors to create the Vref output across an output resistor, and also provides a temperature-compensated current output (Iref) via a cascoded current mirror.
