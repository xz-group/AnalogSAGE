This Kuijk/Banba-style voltage reference employs an opamp to equalize two input nodes, V(va) and V(vb), while a PMOS current mirror sets equal currents into their respective branches. One branch includes a unit BJT, the other an N-times BJT in series with a resistor to develop deltaVBE, and a mirrored output current through a resistor generates the temperature-compensated reference voltage.
