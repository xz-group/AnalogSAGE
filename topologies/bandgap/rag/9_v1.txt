This dual-core current-mode bandgap uses two independent PMOS-input OTA loops: one generates a PTAT current by balancing voltages across a 1x PNP and an 8x PNP with a series resistor, while the other generates a CTAT current across a resistor. The output voltage Vref is created by summing mirrored copies of these PTAT and CTAT currents through an output resistor, and a separate cascoded current output Iref is also provided.
