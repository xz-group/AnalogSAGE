This circuit is a Kuijk-style bandgap reference where a PMOS-input operational transconductance amplifier (OTA) forces equal voltages at the emitters of two PNP transistors (1x and N-times), with a resistor in series with the N-times transistor, establishing a PTAT current. The `vref` output is derived from a resistive divider connected to a replica PNP branch, while the `iref` output is independently generated by summing a CTAT current from another replica PNP with a mirrored PTAT current, subsequently buffered through a cascoded NMOS mirror.
