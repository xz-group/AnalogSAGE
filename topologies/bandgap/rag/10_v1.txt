This voltage-mode bandgap reference uses a PMOS-input OTA to equalize the emitter voltages of a unit PNP BJT and a scaled PNP BJT in series with a resistor, thereby generating a current proportional to absolute temperature (PTAT). This PTAT current is then mirrored into an output resistor to produce the reference voltage, with the `iref` pin directly connected to `vref` and not serving as an independent current output.
