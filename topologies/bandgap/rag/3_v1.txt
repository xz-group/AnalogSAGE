The circuit is a voltage-mode bandgap reference. A PMOS input-pair operational amplifier (OTA) forces the emitter voltage of a unit PNP (Q1) to equal a controlling node voltage in the N-times PNP branch (Q2), establishing a $\Delta V_{BE}$ across a resistor (R1) that generates a PTAT current, which is then combined with CTAT currents from other resistors to form a temperature-compensated core current that is mirrored into an output resistor string to produce the Vref.
