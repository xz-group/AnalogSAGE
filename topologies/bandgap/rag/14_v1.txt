This bandgap reference employs two independent loops: a first error amplifier forces equal voltages at the collectors of a 1x and an 8x PNP BJT pair with an intervening resistor, generating a PTAT (Proportional To Absolute Temperature) component. A second amplifier regulates a CTAT (Complementary To Absolute Temperature) current through a separate resistor, and the `vref` voltage and `iref` current outputs are then formed by summing mirrored copies of these PTAT and CTAT signals.
