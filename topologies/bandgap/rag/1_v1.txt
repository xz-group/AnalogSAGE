This Banba-style current-mode bandgap reference uses PMOS current mirrors to supply currents to two BJT branches, one with an additional series resistor, where an error amplifier regulates node potentials to establish a temperature-dependent core current. This core current is then mirrored to generate the temperature-compensated reference voltage across a composite resistor and a reference current through a cascoded NMOS mirror, with a dedicated startup circuit ensuring robust operation.
