This dual-loop bandgap reference utilizes two separate error amplifiers: one to generate a PTAT current from a 1x/8x PNP BJT pair and a resistor, and another to generate a CTAT current by forcing a resistor voltage to match a PNP's VBE. The final reference voltage is produced by summing mirrored copies of both currents through an output composite resistor, and an active startup circuit ensures proper operation.
