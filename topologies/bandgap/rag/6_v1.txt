This dual-loop self-biased bandgap reference uses two independent OTAs: one balances voltages across a PTAT branch (PNP pair with R1), and the other across a CTAT branch (R2), both referenced to a common node. The combined currents from these amplifiers are mirrored to generate a temperature-compensated reference current (iref) and a reference voltage (vref) across R3, with an integrated startup circuit to ensure proper biasing.
