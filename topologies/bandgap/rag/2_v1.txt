The circuit is a PTAT-core bandgap reference, where a 5T NMOS-input operational transconductance amplifier (OTA) equalizes the voltages at two input branches: one containing a unit PNP and the other an N-times PNP in series with a resistor (R1), thereby establishing a PTAT current. A PMOS current mirror, controlled by the OTA, sources equal currents into these branches, combining PTAT and CTAT components to form a temperature-compensated current that is mirrored to produce both a reference voltage across a resistor (R3) and a cascoded current output.
