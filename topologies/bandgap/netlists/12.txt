* across the 1x/8x PNP pair + Rptat. vref is a ratio-based divider (Rdiva/Rdivb) tapped off a
* separate replica PNP branch (Q3); iref is a genuinely independent output, summing a CTAT

* separation of vref/iref of any converted run. Startup: an RC kick (csu/rsu) plus a weak
* static pulldown (rstartup) pull drB low if the core never turns on.
