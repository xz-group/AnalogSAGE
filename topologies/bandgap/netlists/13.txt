* Dual-loop PTAT/CTAT bandgap (source: claude-sonnet-5, dts21 princeton run 20260716-171123).
* Originally a flat/global-node netlist; the source also defined a helper "RCOMP" composite-
* resistor sub-subcircuit (res_generic_po in series with res_xhigh_po) which is inlined here
* as explicit device pairs since this fixed format uses one flat subckt with no nested
* helper subcircuits. OTA1 (out=vg) forces V(v2)=V(v1) across the 1x/8x PNP pair (PTAT);
* OTA2 (out=vg2) forces V(vb)=V(va) across a lone PNP + resistor (CTAT). vref sums mirrored
* copies of both loop currents through a composite resistor at "vx" (renamed vref). iref was
* originally an unloaded replica of that same mirror pair used only for an AC Rout test
* ("iout"); renamed here to the iref pin since it is a real, separately matched mirror copy.
