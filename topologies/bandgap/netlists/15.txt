* connectivity, so this is transcribed directly from the schematic.
* Opamp forces V(va)=V(vb); PMOS mirror M1/M2/M3 (gates = vc = opamp output) sets three
* equal branch currents. Branch A (va): R1 to gnda in parallel with diode-connected Q1
* (unit). Branch B (vb): R2(=R1) to gnda in parallel with [R0 + diode-connected Q2 (N
* units)] -- dVBE develops across R0. Output branch: M3 -> vref, R3 to gnda -> the
* temperature-compensated bandgap voltage V(vref) = I3*R3.
* This is a pure voltage reference (the figure has no current-output pin); iref is exposed
* only as a 0-ohm alias of vref for 4-pin interface compatibility.
* The opamp is drawn behaviorally (triangle) in the figure and is kept here as an
* 'amplifier' block -- it has NO sky130 primitive; the testbench must supply an opamp
